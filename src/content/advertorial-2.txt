It's just hair.

My makeup was done.

My purse was by the door.

I was ready.

Put some product in it.

Still didn't like it.

No.

"I don't even want to go through this today."

More frizzy.

require.

I started avoiding certain situations.

Not consciously at first.

I'd tell myself I was tired.

Or I didn't feel like going out.

Or I would go later.

I didn't like how my hair looked.

I hated that.

notice.

I knew all of that.

But when I looked in the mirror and saw frizz everywhere, I didn't feel
presentable.

And when I didn't feel presentable, I didn't feel like myself.

That's the part I couldn't explain.

Straightener.

Blow dryer.

Oil.

Smoothing products.

Different shampoos.

Different brushes.

"What am I supposed to do with this?"

I'd smooth one side.

Then notice another.

I'd get the front looking good.

Then realize the back had puffed up.

I'd finally finish and think it looked great.

Humidity.

Wind.

Back to square one.

Because I was constantly thinking about it.

Like I'd actually done something with it.
